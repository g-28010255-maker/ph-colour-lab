pH Colour Lab

Investigate vinegar, plain water and baking-soda solution with a virtual red-cabbage indicator. Make a prediction, test, sort and explain.

  • Hand control: point with your index finger. Pinch thumb and index finger to grab; move to drag; release to drop.
  • Mouse or touch: click and drag the dropper, or tap a sample and use the lab controls.
  • Hover menu: hold your cursor over a menu button for 0.8 seconds.
  • Camera: use HTTPS or localhost and allow camera access. Camera access from file:// is not guaranteed.

Safety: This is a virtual experiment. Never taste laboratory samples. Colour changes are approximate and depend on concentration.
